# Our Villages Rental — Owner / Landlord Guide

_A step-by-step guide for property owners listing on Our Villages Rental.
Everything here happens on the public website — you don't need the WordPress
admin._

> Printable: open this file in your browser and use File → Print / Save as PDF.

---

## 1. Create your account

1. Click **Create Account** (or **List Your Property**) and fill in your details.
2. You'll get a welcome email. Follow the **Welcome** steps to complete your profile.
3. Upload a profile photo from your dashboard (no Gravatar needed).

## 2. Choose a membership

Before you can publish a listing you need an active membership.

1. Go to **Pricing** and pick the plan that fits how many listings you need.
2. Complete checkout. Your membership activates immediately and you'll get a
   confirmation email.
3. Memberships **renew** to keep your listings visible — you'll be emailed before
   expiry. If a membership lapses, your listings are paused (not deleted) until
   you renew.

> No listing without an active paid subscription — this keeps the marketplace
> high-quality.

## 3. Create a listing

From your **Dashboard**, choose **Add Listing**. The editor is organised into
simple sections:

- **General** — title, short description (this becomes the search-result
  summary), full description, property type, bedrooms, bathrooms, guests.
- **Pricing** — choose your pricing model: per-week, per-month, or a fixed block.
  Enter the rate(s). You can hide pricing if you prefer "contact for rates".
- **Location** — address and village. The map pin is generated automatically
  from your address.
- **Photos & Media** — upload photos (drag to reorder, add captions, rotate).
  Optionally add a video and a 360° panorama. The site adds a subtle watermark.
- **Availability** — block out dates, or connect an iCal feed (Airbnb/VRBO) so
  your calendar stays in sync.

Click **Save Changes**. There are no half-saved drafts — what you save is what
you have. New listings may be reviewed before going live; you'll be emailed when
approved (or if something needs fixing).

### Photo tips
- Use bright, landscape (wide) photos — the first photo is your cover image.
- The site keeps the right orientation and creates fast-loading versions
  automatically.

## 4. Manage your listings

From the **Dashboard** you can, per listing:

- **View** the public page.
- **Edit** any detail.
- **Delete** (recoverable for a short grace period if you change your mind).
- **Bump** — push the listing back to the top of search results.

You can also filter and search your own listings when you have several.

## 5. Promote a listing (optional upgrades)

Paid promotions help you stand out:

- **Featured** — a gold highlight and priority placement in search.
- **Bump / Top of Page** — float to the top of results.
- **Homepage Slider** — appear in the homepage featured rail.

Buy these from the listing's upgrade options. Wallet purchases apply instantly;
card purchases activate once payment confirms. Some upgrades are **renewable**
or can **auto-renew** so you don't lose placement.

## 6. Inquiries & reviews

- When a guest sends an **inquiry**, you get an email and can reply from your
  dashboard. The guest also gets a confirmation.
- Your phone number is shown via a **"reveal"** control on the listing — it isn't
  exposed to scrapers.
- Guests can leave **reviews**. Reviews are moderated before they appear. Strong
  reviews also feed the site's testimonials.

## 7. Bookings & calendar

- Reservations and blocked dates appear on your availability calendar.
- **iCal**: import feeds from other platforms and export your OVR calendar so all
  your channels stay consistent and you avoid double-bookings.

## 8. Your dashboard at a glance

- **Overview** — quick stats and recent activity.
- **My Listings** — view / edit / delete / bump / upgrade.
- **Inquiries** — your message pipeline.
- **Profile** — contact details, photo, password.
- **Membership** — your plan and renewal status.

## 9. Tips for more bookings

- Complete every field — listings with photos, clear pricing and a full
  description rank and convert better.
- Keep your calendar current.
- Respond to inquiries quickly — response time is visible to the platform.
- Consider a Featured or Homepage Slider upgrade during peak season.
